PM Brand, PM Editorial, PM Serif and PM Serif Ext are character subsets of
Noto Serif SC, instanced at a fixed weight and renamed for this project.

- PM Brand (pm-brand.woff2): wordmark, weight 600.
- PM Editorial (pm-editorial.woff2): public site and account headings, weight 500.
- PM Serif (pm-serif-core.woff2) and PM Serif Ext (pm-serif-ext.woff2):
  workspace headings, weight 600.
